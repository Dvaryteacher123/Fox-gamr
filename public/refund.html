Welcome to DVARY GAMES. We are committed to providing top-tier digital gaming experiences, instant game downloads, and VIP access features. Please review our refund policy carefully before completing any purchase through our payment systems (FimiPay).

2. Digital Products & Sales Finality

Because all items and VIP privileges offered on DVARY GAMES are digital goods (such as downloadable game files, software packages, and instant unlocking credentials), all sales are final. Once a VIP game code has been issued, unlocked, or downloaded, we cannot offer refunds, returns, or exchanges.

3. Exceptions and Eligible Reversals

Refunds or transaction adjustments will only be reviewed and processed under strict, verified technical conditions:

4. How to Submit a Claim

If you encounter a billing error or experience a technical issue where your paid content was not delivered, please contact our support team immediately. You must provide your transaction ID, order reference number, and payment phone number for verification.
